We Don't Work for Your Competition.

WARNING: Your SEO Agency May Also Be Working for the Businesses You're Trying to Outrank.

This happens more often than many business owners realize.

Many SEO agencies accept clients in the same industry and geographic market. Unless you have a written exclusivity agreement, you shouldn't assume your competitors are off-limits.

Think about that for a moment.

You might be paying an SEO agency thousands of dollars every month to help your company rank #1 in Google.

But what if that same agency is also getting paid by your biggest local competitor to rank #1 for the very same searches?

Who gets their best ideas? Who gets their full attention? And whose business comes first?

That's a question every business owner should be asking.

We Take a Different Approach.

When 24-7 Restoration partnered with White Buffalo Creative, we made a commitment:

We would not provide SEO services to more than 30 specifically identified local restoration competitors covered by our agreement.

We focus on helping 24-7 Restoration compete for the local searches that matter to its business — not simultaneously offering those same SEO services to its protected competitors.

Your competitors shouldn't also be your SEO agency's clients.

That's how we believe local SEO partnerships should work.
